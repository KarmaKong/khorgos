QUOTE CHECK

How to check a Khorgos line quote before accepting it

2026-10-25

When the written Khorgos quote arrives, do not look only at the total. Check validity, the Kazakhstan routing, destination customs and the charges included or excluded. This line has no published day count; ask for timing in the same written response.

  • Routing: Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran
  • No Kyrgyz 0.4% on the Khorgos line
  • Formal quotes come by email with a validity date
  • No Khorgos day count is published

Routing and validity

The quote should name Khorgos, not reuse another line's day table. The corridor runs through Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan to Iran. Keep the RFQ number and validity date. Ask for a revised quote if weight, volume, cargo or destination customs changes.

Included charges

The scope includes full linehaul freight, China-side warehousing and handling, China export declaration, and overseas transit-agency fees. If any item is unclear in the letter, ask before accepting.

Excluded charges

Destination clearance, insurance, inspection, securing or reinforcement, lifting, extra overseas costs and any over-limit portion are separate. The Kyrgyz 0.4% is a Wuqia-line tax and is not added to Khorgos.

Delivery and clearance

Match the destination customs point with the quote. Iran entry is via Sarakhs. Delivery is hub to customs under CPT / DAP, not door-to-door or DDP. Iran clearance belongs to the buyer and their broker.

FAQ

Does the Khorgos quote include transit days?

No day count is published on the page. Ask for timing in the written response for that shipment.

Is the Kyrgyz 0.4% added?

No. That tax applies only on the Wuqia line.

Is Iran clearance included?

No. The buyer and their broker handle Iran clearance.
