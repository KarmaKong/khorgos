Leaving Khorgos: the Kazakhstan corridor, without the Kyrgyz 0.4%

2026-10-21

This page is the Khorgos exit. The truck leaves Xinjiang at Khorgos, runs Kazakhstan, then Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan, and ends at Iran customs. Starting at Khorgos is the corridor; this page is the tax that does not apply here.

  • Routing: Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran
  • No Kyrgyz 0.4% transit tax on this line
  • No Khorgos transit days on the page
  • Sellable destinations: Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs. Entry via Sarakhs

Why the 0.4% is not here

The truck does not transit Kyrgyzstan. That charge is a tax, not freight, and it belongs to Wuqia. It is not added here. The tax base is not stated on the page.

Do not paste the Wuqia day table

No Khorgos transit days are published. Ask for them on a written quote. Torugart and Alashankou are not operated.

Handover

Sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs. Entry is Sarakhs. The buyer clears in Iran. CPT / DAP, hub to customs yard. Not door-to-door, not DDP. Curtainsider, not a container.

The enquiry

Three things: origin hub, destination customs (Mashhad or Tehran), cargo data (HS, gross weight, CBM). Missing one, no written rate. No rates on the page.

FAQ

No. This corridor is Kazakhstan.

No. Destination customs yard. CPT / DAP. The buyer clears in Iran.
