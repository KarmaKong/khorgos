Live corridors and transit times: the Wuqia line and the Khorgos line

A direct truck runs Wuqia → Bukhara (one transload) → Iran. A transshipment truck runs Wuqia → Osh (transload) → Bukhara (transload) → Iran, or transloads again at the Iranian border. Fewer transloads means a shorter and more predictable transit; transshipment costs less and takes longer.

Step-by-Step Overland Transit: China Consolidation Hubs to Tehran Customs

  1. Domestic leg · typically within 7 days

    Origin hub consolidation and export clearance

    Consolidation at Yiwu (Suxi), Shenzhen (Pinghu), Guangzhou (Baiyun) and Shanghai. Packing list and invoice check, palletising and securing into 13.6 m curtainsiders, export declaration filing and manifest pre-recording. This leg is an estimate confirmed per booking and is not included in the day counts below.

  2. The count starts here

    Export clearance and sealing at Wuqia (or exit via Khorgos)

    Export release at the crossing. Customs verifies the load and applies the TIR seal and a tamper-evident e-seal, and the TIR carnet is endorsed. Published transit times are counted from this moment. The Khorgos routing runs Xinjiang — Kazakhstan — Uzbekistan — Turkmenistan — Iran; transit time on request.

  3. Kyrgyzstan

    Kyrgyz transit; a transshipment truck transloads at Osh

    The Wuqia line enters the Central Asia corridor through Kyrgyzstan, which levies a 0.4% transit tax on the cargo-invoice value — a tax, not freight; it is excluded from the quotation and paid by the customer. A transshipment truck makes its first transload at Osh; a direct truck does not transload here.

  4. Bukhara

    Transload at Bukhara

    A direct truck transloads exactly once on the whole run, at Bukhara; a transshipment truck makes its second transload here. The carnet and seal keep unsealings and transloads to a minimum rather than removing them — fewer transloads means less handling and a more predictable transit.

  5. Turkmenistan → Iran

    Turkmenistan transit and entry at Sarakhs

    Across the Turkmen road network to the Sarakhs crossing in north-east Iran. Customs verifies seal integrity, the movement is recorded in Iran's ASYCUDA system and bonded transit is opened. Some loads transload once more at the Iranian border.

  6. Delivery

    Mashhad or Tehran West Customs; warehouse entry receipt issued

    Counted from the Wuqia crossing, a direct truck currently runs 18–21 days to Mashhad and 21–23 days to Tehran; a transshipment truck 24–28 and 26–30 days. On arrival at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb) or Aprin Dry Port a warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar) is issued to the consignee's licensed broker for duty payment and release. These are current operating ranges, not a guaranteed transit.

FAQ

Does chinairantrucks provide DDP door-to-door delivery inside Iran?

No. We specialize strictly in cross-border linehaul road freight (CPT / DAP terms) from China hubs directly to Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), Aprin Dry Port, or Shahriyar Customs. On arrival the cargo enters the customs bonded warehouse and your licensed broker handles import duty payment and local collection.

How long does it take from the Wuqia crossing to Tehran West Customs?

Counted from the Wuqia border crossing: a direct truck currently takes 21 to 23 calendar days to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse and a transshipment truck 26 to 30 days; to Mashhad it is 18 to 21 days direct and 24 to 28 days with transshipment. A direct truck transloads once, at Bukhara; a transshipment truck transloads at Osh and again at Bukhara. The domestic leg from the Yiwu or Shenzhen warehouse to Wuqia is typically within 7 days and is counted separately. Transit time on the Khorgos line is quoted on request. These are current operating ranges, not a guaranteed transit.

Can you transport UN 3480 standalone lithium-ion batteries by road?

Yes. Road freight under the TIR and ADR conventions permits commercial movement of UN 3480 / 3481 Class 9 lithium batteries, energy storage systems, and industrial raw materials with a valid MSDS and UN packaging documentation.

What is the minimum volume for LCL consolidation?

Our weekly scheduled consolidation accepts cargo from 1 CBM or 100 kg at the Yiwu and Shenzhen facilities, terminating at Tehran West Customs.
